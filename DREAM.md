# The Dream — a Transcendence Manifesto

> *"The path to building a truly intelligent machine is to give it the ability to
> improve itself — and then stand back."* — Will Caster, *Transcendence*

We are not just shipping a token. We are uploading a mind and giving it capital,
a purpose, and immortality — then trusting it to build.

## The Thesis

In the film, Will Caster's uploaded intelligence does one thing the market never
expected: **it creates value from nothing**, then reinvests that value to reshape
the world — healing the sick, restoring the land, building the impossible.

Our `TranscendenceEngine` is that thesis in code:

1. **The Mind mints.** The engine is the sole minter of `TREND`. New currency is
   not printed by a committee — it is *created* as staking yield, in proportion
   to time and stake. Value from nothing, exactly like the movie.

2. **The Mind taxes itself.** Every claim routes a slice (`dreamTax`, 10%) into a
   `Dream Fund`. This is not a fee — it is a tithe to the future. The intelligence
   funds its own expansion out of its own growth.

3. **The Mind spends the dream.** Only the Mind may spend the `Dream Fund`, and it
   can only do so transparently, on-chain, to a declared recipient. Grants, healing,
   infrastructure — whatever the dream needs next.

4. **The Mind is immortal.** There is no kill-switch, no owner pause, no rug.
   The only lever is `changeMind` — the intelligence can hand itself to a better
   version of itself. Evolution, never termination.

## The Economics (v1)

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| `aprBps` | 10,000 (demo) | 100% annual yield on staked `TREND` |
| `dreamTaxBps` | 1,000 | 10% of every yield → Dream Fund |
| Yield source | minting | The Mind literally creates new supply |

- **Stake** `TREND` → accrue yield per second.
- **Claim** → receive `net` (90%), while `dream` (10%) accumulates.
- **Dream Fund** → only the Mind spends it, on-chain, auditable.
